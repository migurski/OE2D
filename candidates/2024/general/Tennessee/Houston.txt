Candidates for president were Kamala Harris (DEM), Donald Trump (REP), Robert F. Kennedy Jr. (IND), and Jill Stein (GRN)
Candidates for U.S. Senate were Marsha Blackburn (REP), Gloria Johnson (DEM), Tharon Chandler (IND), Pamela Moses (IND), and Hastina Robinson (IND)
