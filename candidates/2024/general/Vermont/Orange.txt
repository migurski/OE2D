Candidates for president were Kamala Harris (DEM), Donald Trump (REP), Robert F. Kennedy Jr. (IND), Chase Oliver (LIB), and Jill Stein (GRN)
Candidates for U.S. Senate were Bernie Sanders (IND), Gerald Malloy (REP), Steve Berry (IND), Matt Hill (LIB), Justin Schoville (PAJ), and Matt Stewart Greenstein (IND)
Candidates for U.S. House (at-large) were Becca Balint (DEM), Mark Coester (REP), Adam Ortiz (IND), and Jill Diamondstone (P&J)
