Candidates for Straight Party were Democratic Party (DEM), Republican Party (REP), Libertarian Party (LIB), Green Party (GRN), Constitution Party (CST), U.S. Taxpayers Party (UST), Natural Law Party (NLP), and Working Class Party (WCP)
Candidates for president were Kamala Harris (DEM), Donald Trump (REP), Jill Stein (GRN), Robert F. Kennedy Jr. (NLP), and Chase Oliver (LIB)
Candidates for U.S. Senate were Elissa Slotkin (DEM), Mike Rogers (REP), Joseph Solis-Mullen (LIB), Douglas Marsh (GRN), Dave Stein (UST), and Doug Dern (NLP)
Candidates for U.S. House District 2 were John Moolenaar (REP), Michael Lynch (DEM), Ben DeJong (LIB), and Scott Adams (UST)
Candidates for U.S. House District 8 were Kristen McDonald Rivet (DEM), Paul Junge (REP), Kathy Goodwin (WCP), Steve Barcelo (LIB), James Little (UST), and Jim Casha (GRN)
