Candidates for Straight Party were Democratic Party (DEM), Republican Party (REP), Libertarian Party (LIB), Green Party (GRN), Constitution Party (CST), U.S. Taxpayers Party (UST), Natural Law Party (NLP), and Working Class Party (WCP)
Candidates for president were Kamala Harris (DEM), Donald Trump (REP), and Chase Oliver (LIB)
Candidates for U.S. Senate were Bernie Moreno (REP), Sherrod Brown (DEM), and Don Kissick (LIB)
Candidates for U.S. House District 10 were Mike Turner (REP), Amy Cox (DEM), and Michael Harbaugh (IND)
Candidates for U.S. House District 15 were Mike Carey (REP) and Adam Miller (DEM)
