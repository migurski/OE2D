Candidates for U.S. Senate were Chris Murphy (DEM), Matthew Corey (REP), Robert F. Hyde (IND), and Justin Paglino (GRN)
Candidates for U.S. House District 1 were John B. Larson (DEM), Jim Griffin (REP), and Mary Sanders (GRN)
