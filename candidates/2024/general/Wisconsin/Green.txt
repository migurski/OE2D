Candidates for Straight Party were Democratic Party (DEM), Republican Party (REP), Libertarian Party (LIB), Green Party (GRN), Constitution Party (CST), U.S. Taxpayers Party (UST), Natural Law Party (NLP), and Working Class Party (WCP)
Candidates for president were Kamala Harris (DEM), Donald Trump (REP), Robert F. Kennedy Jr. (IND), Jill Stein (GRN), and Chase Oliver (LIB)
Candidates for U.S. Senate were Tammy Baldwin (DEM), Eric Hovde (REP), Phil Anderson (DTC), and Thomas Leager (AF)
Candidates for U.S. House District 2 were Mark Pocan (DEM) and Erik Olsen (REP)
