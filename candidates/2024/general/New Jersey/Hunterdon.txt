Candidates for Straight Party were Democratic Party (DEM), Republican Party (REP), Libertarian Party (LIB), Green Party (GRN), Constitution Party (CST), U.S. Taxpayers Party (UST), Natural Law Party (NLP), and Working Class Party (WCP)
Candidates for president were Kamala Harris (DEM), Donald Trump (REP), Jill Stein (GRN), Robert F. Kennedy Jr. (IND), and Chase Oliver (LIB)
Candidates for U.S. Senate were Andy Kim (DEM), Curtis Bashaw (REP), Christina Khalil (GRN), Ken Kaplan (LIB), Patricia Mooneyham (IND), and Joanne Kuniansky (SW)
Candidates for U.S. House District 7 were Tom Kean Jr. (REP), Sue Altman (DEM), Andrew Black (GRN), and Lana Leguia (LIB)
