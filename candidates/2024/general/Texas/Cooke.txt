Candidates for Straight Party were Democratic Party (DEM), Republican Party (REP), Libertarian Party (LIB), Green Party (GRN), Constitution Party (CST), U.S. Taxpayers Party (UST), Natural Law Party (NLP), and Working Class Party (WCP)
Candidates for president were Kamala Harris (DEM), Donald Trump (REP), Jill Stein (GRN), and Chase Oliver (LIB)
Candidates for U.S. Senate were Ted Cruz (REP), Colin Allred (DEM), and Ted Brown (LIB)
Candidates for U.S. House District 26 were Brandon Gill (REP), Ernest Lineberger III (DEM), and Phil Gray (LIB)
